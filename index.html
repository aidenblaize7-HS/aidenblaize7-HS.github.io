shortsscout

shortsscout is a private, single-owner tool that reads performance statistics of the owner's own YouTube channels (views and average view percentage per video) and writes them to the owner's private workspace for content planning.

Its uploader component uploads the owner's own videos to the owner's own channels only after the owner has reviewed and approved each one. It is not offered to the public; only the owner of the channels signs in.
